Shipping Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Free delivery across India on every order. Shipping is included in the price you see.

Processing time

Every item is printed to order. Ready designs are usually dispatched within 3-5 working days of payment. Custom and bulk orders are dispatched after design approval, on the timeline we confirm with you.

Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3-7 working days depending on your location. We ship through trusted courier partners via Shiprocket.

Tracking

You receive the tracking link by WhatsApp, SMS or email once your order is dispatched.

Delivery address

Please make sure your address and phone number are correct. If a parcel is returned because of a wrong address or because no one was available after repeated attempts, re-shipping may be charged.
