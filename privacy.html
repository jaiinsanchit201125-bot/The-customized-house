Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how The Customized House, a brand of ARIHANTH SELECTIONS (GSTIN 29AFUPK2861E1Z2), collects and uses your information when you visit thecustomizedhouse.com or order from us.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Designs, photos and text you send us for custom products.
  • Your UPI transaction ID or payment screenshot, used only to confirm your payment. We never ask for or store your UPI PIN, card or bank details.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data from your browser (such as pages visited) to improve the website.

How we use it

  • To make, ship and support your order, and to send order and delivery updates by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
  • To issue GST invoices and keep the records required by law.
  • Photos uploaded to the virtual try-on are used only to create your preview and are not stored by us.

Who we share it with

Only with the partners needed to complete your order: our website and store platform (Hostinger) and our courier partners (through Shiprocket). We never sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time, except records we must keep for tax purposes. Write to shop@thecustomizedhouse.com.
