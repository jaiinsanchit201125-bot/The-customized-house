FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I order just one piece?

Yes. We make single custom pieces as well as bulk orders for batches, teams and companies.

Do I need my own design?

No. Send us your idea, names or a reference picture and we create the design and a free mockup for you.

How do I pay?

After you approve the mockup we confirm the final price on WhatsApp. For bulk orders we take an advance to start and the balance on delivery.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the product and quantity. We confirm the delivery date with you before we start, so you know exactly when to expect it.

Can every person in a group get their own name?

Yes. Send us the list of names and sizes and we prepare a separate print file for each person.

Will the print fade?

We use DTF printing and embroidery made for regular wear. Wash inside out in cold water and avoid ironing directly on the print.
